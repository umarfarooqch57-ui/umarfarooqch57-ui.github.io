BikeLog Privacy Policy

BikeLog: Motorcycle Tracker. Effective October 7, 2026.

BikeLog helps you track fuel, maintenance, and renewal dates for your motorcycle. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. In short: your bike records stay on your phone, and the only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service uses to show ads.

Information you enter

The details you add in BikeLog, including your bike's name and model, odometer readings, fuel entries, service records, renewal dates, notes, and your unit and currency settings, are stored only on your device. BikeLog has no accounts and no servers, and the developer cannot see or access any of this information.

Android may include app data in your device's backup to your Google account, depending on your phone's backup settings. That backup is managed by Google and your device, not by BikeLog.

Reminders and notifications

Oil change, renewal, and odometer reminders are created on your device from the information you entered. They are not sent from a server. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

Advertising

BikeLog is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and information about how ads are viewed and tapped. Your bike records are never shared with Google or any advertiser.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, BikeLog asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can accept, refuse, or choose specific options, and you can change your choice later from Settings > Privacy choices in the app. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. To learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to show your oil change, renewal, and odometer reminders.
  • Internet access: used only to load ads and handle ad consent.

BikeLog does not use your location, camera, contacts, or files.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries inside the app. To delete everything, clear BikeLog's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app. Because your records are stored only on your device, there is nothing for the developer to delete on their side.

Children

BikeLog is intended for adults who own or ride motorcycles. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
