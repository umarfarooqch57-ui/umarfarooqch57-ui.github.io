Imla Practice privacy policy

Tinvale Apps. Effective October 14, 2026.

Imla Practice keeps your family's information on your phone. We don't have accounts or servers, and we never see the names, word lists or scores you enter. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising service needs to show ads.

Who we are

Imla Practice is an app that helps parents practise Urdu and English spelling (imla) with their children. It is made by Tinvale Apps, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Tinvale Apps and "you" means the person using the app.

Information you enter, kept only on your phone

To work, the app stores the following in a database on your device:

  • Children's names (or nicknames) that you add
  • Word lists you create
  • Practice results: which words were marked correct or wrong, and when
  • Your app settings, such as speech speed and default language

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no way to access it. It is deleted when you delete a child in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Voice

Words are read aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone, such as Speech Services by Google. Imla Practice uses the voices stored on your device and does not record your voice or your child's voice. The app does not use the microphone.

Advertising

Imla Practice is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and general location, and information about how ads are viewed and tapped. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, and you can learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The app only requests family-friendly ads (content rated G), and ads are never shown on the dictation or marking screens.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings under Google, then Ads, or Privacy, then Ads, depending on your device.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent before ads use your data, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings, then Ad privacy settings.

Children

Imla Practice is designed to be used by parents and other adults, together with a child. Children's names and results are entered by the adult and stay on the device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not ask children to create accounts or provide information.

Internet access

The app works without internet for practice. It uses the internet only to load ads and to show the consent form where required.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes on Google Play.
