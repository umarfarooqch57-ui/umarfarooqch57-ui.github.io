Plant Care – Privacy Policy

Developer: Tinvale Apps · Last updated: October 2026

Plant Care gives you daily, weather-aware care advice for your plants. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: there is no account, your plants stay on your phone, and only what is needed for weather and photo identification ever leaves it.

What stays on your phone

Your plants, their names and photos, your care history (watering, feeding, pruning), your chosen city, units and reminder settings are stored only on your device. We do not have a server that receives this information, and we cannot see it.

If you have turned on Android's backup on your phone, Android may include this app data in your personal device backup with Google, under your Google account and Google's terms. You control this in your phone's settings.

What leaves your phone, and why

Location

You can choose your city by searching, or tap “Use my location”. Only then does the app ask for location permission. It reads your location once, turns it into a city, and keeps only the city and its rounded coordinates. The app never tracks your location in the background.

Camera and photos

The app uses your phone's camera app or photo picker only when you tap “Take a photo” or “Choose photo”. It does not ask for camera or storage permissions. Photos you add to your plants stay on your phone. A photo is sent off the phone only when you ask the app to identify a plant.

Notifications

If you turn on the morning reminder, the app creates the notification on your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

Advertising

The free version of the app may show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location based on IP address, and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the UK, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-up, email or phone number.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not build profiles about you.

Children

The app is a general-audience gardening app and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Removing a plant in the app deletes it, its history and its photo. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything the app stored.

Third-party services

Weather data from MET Norway (licensed under CC BY 4.0). The image-based plant species identification service used, is based on the Pl@ntNet recognition API, regularly updated and accessible through the site https://my.plantnet.org/. Plant Care is not an official Pl@ntNet app.

Changes
